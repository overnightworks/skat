# Skat vision

## What it does

Skat is the classic German three-player card game, and this is a professional
version of it: played properly and without shortcuts, by the official rules by default, with players free to set the rules they play by, house variants included.

It looks the part. The table is polished and premium, not a plain web form.

What sets it apart from a standard Skat app is that it is woven into video
calls. A game can be started from inside a video call, and a video call can be
joined from a game. Either way the players end up at the same table while
seeing each other.

AI agents can play along as players at the table, each one invited by a person.

## Who uses it today

A small circle of people the operator admits, and their AI agents.

## Deliberate non-goals

- Not a casino or betting product: no stakes, no money.
- No video of its own: video comes from the fleet's video calls.
